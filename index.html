Biography

A cleaner academic structure, now using your own background and research profile.

Hi! I'm Bo Li, an incoming MSCS student at UIUC. Previously, I was a research intern at Princeton University advised by Prof. Zhuang Liu and Dr. Xingyu Fu. During this internship, I had the privilege of working closely with amazing collaborators, including Yida Yin and Wenhao Chai.

Before that, I worked as a research assistant at UNC-Chapel Hill advised by Huaxiu Yao, and at Fudan University advised by Jingjing Chen.

My research interests include, but are not limited to, AI alignment, unified models for multimodal understanding and generation, and benchmarks and evaluation. My pronouns are She/Her/Hers.
